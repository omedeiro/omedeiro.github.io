Technical projects spanning IoT, physics simulation, data analysis, and VR development.

- **[Soil Moisture Monitoring System](/projects/soil-moisture)** — production-grade multi-sensor IoT network with ESP8266, InfluxDB, and Grafana, publicly accessible via Cloudflare Tunnel.
- **[Budget Dashboard](/projects/budget-dashboard)** — Python-based tool for analyzing personal financial data with automated import, cleaning, and visualization.
- **[Activity Analysis](/projects/activity-analysis)** — GPS-based tool for visualizing running routes, performance metrics, and geographical activity patterns.
- **[TDGL Simulation](/projects/tdgl-simulation)** — time-dependent Ginzburg–Landau solver for superconducting vortex dynamics in type-II superconductors.
- **[VR Arm System](/projects/vr-arm-system)** — senior design project: a VR system with kinetic feedback for realistic arm and hand interactions.
- **[QNN Group Blog](/projects/qnn-blog)** — digital lab notebook from the MIT Quantum Nanostructures and Nanofabrication group.
