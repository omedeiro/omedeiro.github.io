Computational explorations of mathematical concepts, with the source code behind each one.

- **[Aperiodic Tiles](/maths/aperiodic-tiles)** — Penrose, Ammann–Beenker and hat tilings, built in the browser and explored by pan and zoom.
- **[Bertrand's Paradox](/maths/bertrand-paradox)** — a geometric probability problem demonstrating how different interpretations of "random" can yield different results for the same question.
- **[Euler Spiral](/maths/euler-spiral)** — a mathematical curve where curvature changes linearly with arc length, also known as a clothoid or Cornu spiral.

Bertrand and Euler are MATLAB simulations with the complete source and resulting figures; the tiling page runs live.
